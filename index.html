How it works

Three clicks from
meme to mint.

The alpha doesn't wait for you to open a launchpad, connect a wallet, and re-type a name you already had in your clipboard. WIRE removes all of that.

01 See it

Spot the meme

A post, a chart, a Discord screenshot. Whatever is about to be a coin is already on your screen — you don't leave it.

02 Click it

The panel drops in over the page and pre-fills name, ticker and image from what's under it. Edit anything you don't like.

Launch & share

Mint, metadata and the liquidity pool go out in one bundle. You get a contract address and a share link before the thread cools.

Why a bookmarklet

Smaller than a favicon.
Faster than a launchpad.

Extensions ask for permission to read every page you visit. Launchpad sites ask you to leave the one you're on. A bookmarklet does neither.

Sub-30 second deploys

Mint account, metadata upload and pool creation are bundled into a single signed transaction and pushed through a dedicated RPC with priority fees already tuned. No polling, no "pending" screen.

FAQ

Reasonable questions.

Is this a browser extension?

No. A bookmarklet is a bookmark whose URL is a small JavaScript program. It has no background process, no install, no permissions, and it only runs on a page when you click it. That's the entire security model.

Does it read the pages I visit?

Only the page you're on, only at the moment you click it, and only to autofill a name and image into the form. Nothing is sent anywhere until you sign a transaction.

Do I need to connect a wallet?

For a real launch, yes — any Solana wallet with a browser adapter. WIRE builds the transaction; your wallet approves it. There's no deposit step and no account to create.

What does it cost?

1% of the initial liquidity you seed, taken at launch, plus the network's own rent and priority fees. No subscription and no listing charge.

Which chains are supported?

Solana at launch. Base and Hyperliquid are next in the queue — the panel is chain-agnostic, only the transaction builder swaps out.

Is the button on this page live?

It's the real panel running in simulation mode: the flow, fields and confirmation are genuine, but no transaction is signed or broadcast. Drag it and try it — nothing can go wrong.

Can I edit the token after it's live?

Metadata stays mutable for 10 minutes after the mint so you can fix a typo or swap the image, then authority is burned automatically.

What happens if WIRE goes offline?

Tokens you've already launched are ordinary SPL mints with ordinary pools — they don't depend on us. The bookmarklet itself keeps working for as long as its RPC endpoint answers.
